Privacy

Playing never needs an account. Signing in is optional and only keeps your progress on every device and your scores on the leaderboards.

Progress, drafts and settings live in your browser. The game's own usage counts and error reports are disabled on public sites.

Anonymous counts and feedback

The game itself sends us no automatic usage counts or error reports, including when an older saved setting enabled them.

Pages are served through Cloudflare, which counts page visits and load speed with its Web Analytics script. Cloudflare says the script uses no cookies or browser storage and doesn't fingerprint visitors. Our own server keeps no log of page requests.

Feedback is your choice. It saves only your 1 to 5 rating, optional comment, the page or problem, game version and date. It is anonymous even when you are signed in. Please leave out personal details. Counts are kept for 13 months and feedback for 2 years, then cleanup removes them from the database and retained backups.

What we store when you sign in

  • Your sign-in provider's id for you: the account number Google or GitHub gives this game. It is how we recognise you next time.
  • A display name: your first name from Google or your GitHub name. You can change it anytime in the account menu. It appears on leaderboards and on "beat my design" links you share.
  • Your avatar's web address, so your picture shows in the account menu.
  • Your progress: solved problems, best scores, finished lessons and the days you played (for your streak).
  • Your scores: your best score per daily challenge and per problem, with its stars.
  • A sign-in cookie that keeps you signed in for 30 days. The server keeps only a scrambled (hashed) copy of it.
  • A one-time sign-in code, only while you sign in with Google: a cookie that lasts at most 10 minutes and is deleted as soon as you are signed in. It makes sure the sign-in really came from your browser.

What we never store

  • Your email address or password.
  • A GitHub access token. GitHub sign-in asks for no permissions beyond your public profile, and the one-time token is thrown away right after reading it.
  • Advertising trackers or a player identity attached to usage counts or feedback.

Who sees what

Anyone can see the leaderboards: display name, score, stars and rank. Your progress is visible only to you. Nothing is sold, rented or shared with anyone.

Delete it anytime

Open the account menu in the top bar and choose Delete my account. Your name, avatar address, synced progress and scores are removed from the server at once. Nightly backups roll off within 7 days. Progress saved on your own device stays there until you clear it in Settings.
